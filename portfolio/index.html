    About

    University of Victoria
    Coquitlam, British Columbia

    If a side effect is possible, a deterministic check should be able to refuse it.

    I study electrical engineering at the University of Victoria and keep a public shelf of small kits under mundaneb3at. Most of them are not product demos. They are readable tools: a verifier, a ledger, a terminal board, a study sim that still runs with the network off.

    The through-line is operational discipline. Assistants are useful when something else can recompute the claim, quarantine an untrusted note, or show its work. The repositories stay dependency-light on purpose — PowerShell where the machine is Windows, the Python standard library when the point is that it should run anywhere.
