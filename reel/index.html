How it's made

The reel's own pipeline diagram, in the four looks the video wipes through.

  1. voice · edge-ttsin: the script. out: one voice track, 57.65 s (en-US-BrianNeural).
  2. words · whisperin: the voice. out: a time for every word; all 150 of 150 script words matched.
  3. cues · Claudein: the word times. out: scene cues anchored to phrases, not fixed seconds, so a new voice still lines up.
  4. scene · Canvasin: cues, AI clips, stills, sim frames. out: every 1080x1920 frame, drawn in code.
  5. render · ffmpegin: frames and voice. out: the 60 s video, h264 + aac.
